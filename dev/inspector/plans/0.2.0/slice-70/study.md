## Participant task cards

1. T01: Describe the stored categories and what each contains. Distinguish stored
   content from generated representations and say what your overview counts cover.
2. T02: Find a survey participant through content rather than a supplied identity.
   Inspect complete structured content, exact large numeric value and a directed
   relationship; return to the original content without reconstructing the search.
3. T03: Inspect that record's indexed text and vector associations. Run text and
   semantic retrieval, inspect a stored-distance neighborhood and graph. Describe
   which operations and measured values answer which questions.
4. T04: Pin two actual observations with some shared and some different results.
   Compare union/intersection, open exact origins and original observations. Identify
   distinct equal-content people and retained revisions without merging identities.
5. T05: Explain separately what is stored, retrieval-ready, permitted in this
   session and selected by this request. For an unreturned record, say what evidence
   establishes its omission cause and what remains unknown.
6. High INS07: Trace one person through recorded source/dependency and retained
   revisions, plus one unavailable recorded endpoint. Explain the
   evidence and distinguish an unresolved target from an empty stored value.
7. High INS08: Retain a retrieval observation, then inspect later readiness and
   eligibility evidence. Explain what each observation establishes without treating
   them as one snapshot or inventing the reason for an absent result.
