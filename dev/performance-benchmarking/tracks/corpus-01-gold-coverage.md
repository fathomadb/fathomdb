# CORPUS-01 — Agent-memory gold coverage

**Status:** planned; prepare in parallel with LOCOMO-01.

## Decision

Is the gold portfolio sufficient to support claims about mutable, temporal,
and provenance-preserving personal memory rather than LOCOMO retrieval alone?

## Preparation and contract

1. Create a corpus matrix covering LOCOMO, LongMemEval, TimelineQA/TimeQA/ToT,
   IR-C/BEIR, MuSiQue, and global-sensemaking data with license and payload rules.
2. Define human-reviewed gold for knowledge update, supersession, source erasure,
   and time-scoped validity; do not manufacture answer oracles.
3. State power, class counts, intended metric, and the claim each corpus can and
   cannot support. Keep non-redistributable material external.

## Exit evidence

The versioned corpus matrix and manifests identify every unsupported claim. A
broad agent-memory claim remains blocked until its required gold category is
adequately represented.
