# FathomDB 0.8.26 prework — Slices 0–7

## Boundary

Except for the requested branch/worktree and draft documents, Slices 0–6 are
read-only investigations. They do not change product code, tests, dependencies,
accepted contracts, architecture authority, public docs, or repository
contents under review. Slice 7 implements only items explicitly selected by
the Slice 6 HITL session.
