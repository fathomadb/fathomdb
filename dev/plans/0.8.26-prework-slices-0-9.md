# FathomDB 0.8.26 prework — Slices 0–9

## Boundary

Except for the requested branch/worktree and draft documents, Slices 0–8 are
read-only investigations and planning. They do not change product code, tests,
dependencies, accepted contracts, architecture authority, public docs, build
infrastructure, or CI/CD configuration. Slice 9 implements only items selected
by the Slice 8 HITL session and allocated to pre-feature preparation.

Build or delivery findings whose correct fix depends on implemented features
may be assigned to a reserved slice after Slice 10 or to Slice 50 instead of
Slice 9. Deferral must be explicit; it is not omission.

## Completion rule

Prework closes only when all proposals from Slices 0–7 have durable
dispositions and delivery placement, the HITL has approved the release
boundary and reviewed the Slice 9 plan, Slice 9 has landed or been explicitly
declared unnecessary, and the release plan points to Slice 10.
