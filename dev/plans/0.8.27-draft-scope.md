# FathomDB 0.8.27 draft scope

## Authority

This file records one reported release blocker and preserves the read-continuity
work deferred by the approved 0.8.25 scope adjustment. It is not an
implementation or publication authorization. The future 0.8.27 planning cycle
must reproduce and disposition the blocker, revalidate every candidate against
the shipped 0.8.25/0.8.26 contracts, and may simplify or postpone a candidate
with a durable reason.

## Reported release blocker

### F27-01 — source erasure after correction/supersession

**Status:** reported by Memex against registry FathomDB 0.8.26; FathomDB-native
reproduction and root-cause analysis are required when the 0.8.27 release plan
opens. Treat this as release-blocking until it is reproduced and resolved or
disproved with durable evidence.

Memex reports that a revision-pinned correction can leave a source bucket
unerasable. The correction supersedes the original revision and writes a live
replacement. A later `Engine.erase_source(bucket)` raises `StorageError` even
after the associated dependency closures report `complete`; the original body
and other records in the bucket remain stored and retrievable.

Observed cases:

- with a same-bucket replacement, the bucket remains locked and unerasable;
- with a replacement in another bucket, erasing the replacement bucket first
  can unlock erasure of the original bucket; and
- Memex reports `storage_failed`, preserving the existing fail-loud erasure
  contract instead of claiming success or using private database APIs.

Impact and placement:

- user-requested privacy deletion cannot be guaranteed for corrected material;
- a caller with correction authority could accidentally or deliberately make a
  bucket unerasable;
- this blocks Memex 0.6.0 production cutover, but does not block continued
  isolated Memex Slice 80 development; and
- this corrective work precedes the optional D27 candidates below.

Required 0.8.27 outcome:

- provide a supported operation that erases a bucket containing superseded
  revisions and closed dependents;
- preserve a truthful erasure report, including any incomplete outcome;
- cover same-bucket and cross-bucket replacement cases with tests; and
- do not require Memex or another SDK consumer to use private database APIs.

Primary external evidence: Memex `release/0.6.0`,
`dev/fathomdb/0.8.26-erase-after-supersede-gap.md`; local checkout at
`/home/coreyt/projects/memex-worktrees/release-0.6.0/dev/fathomdb/0.8.26-erase-after-supersede-gap.md`.

## Candidate scope

| ID | Candidate work | Required first proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D27-01 | Full opt-in cross-operation frozen-snapshot lease with typed mismatch, expiry, unavailable, and drift outcomes. | Show that the compact optional 0.8.25 frozen read cannot meet a concrete multi-operation consistency need. |
| D27-02 | Request-, snapshot-, projection-generation-, and ordering-bound opaque cursor protocol. | Demonstrate duplicate/omission or authorization risk under the minimal 0.8.25 continuation. |
| D27-03 | Generalized graph pagination and richer `operational_state` continuation. | Confirm a bounded consumer workload and deterministic order; keep ranked top-K separate. |
| D27-04 | Persisted source-complete evidence receipts with eligibility-bound replay and retention/expiry behavior. | Demonstrate a need to resolve evidence beyond the compact 0.8.25 reference lifetime without weakening current authorization. |

## Constraints

- All features remain opt-in; ordinary search must not acquire lease overhead.
- No cursor or evidence handle may reveal bytes after visibility, eligibility,
  lifecycle, or erasure changes.
- Public state stays compact and versioned. Cryptographic signing is required
  only if the threat model and deployment boundary justify it.
- The release must use its normal prework and dependency-ordered slice plan.
