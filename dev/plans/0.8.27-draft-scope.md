# FathomDB 0.8.27 draft scope

## Authority

This file preserves the read-continuity work deferred by the approved 0.8.25
scope adjustment. It is not an implementation or publication authorization.
The future 0.8.27 planning cycle must revalidate every item against the shipped
0.8.25/0.8.26 contracts and may simplify or postpone it with a durable reason.

## Candidate scope

| ID | Candidate work | Required first proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D27-01 | Full opt-in cross-operation frozen-snapshot lease with typed mismatch, expiry, unavailable, and drift outcomes. | Show that the compact optional 0.8.25 frozen read cannot meet a concrete multi-operation consistency need. |
| D27-02 | Request-, snapshot-, projection-generation-, and ordering-bound opaque cursor protocol. | Demonstrate duplicate/omission or authorization risk under the minimal 0.8.25 continuation. |
| D27-03 | Generalized graph pagination and richer `operational_state` continuation. | Confirm a bounded consumer workload and deterministic order; keep ranked top-K separate. |
| D27-04 | Persisted source-complete evidence receipts with eligibility-bound replay and retention/expiry behavior. | Demonstrate a need to resolve evidence beyond the compact 0.8.25 reference lifetime without weakening current authorization. |

## Constraints

- All features remain opt-in; ordinary search must not acquire lease overhead.
- No cursor or evidence handle may reveal bytes after visibility, eligibility,
  lifecycle, or erasure changes.
- Public state stays compact and versioned. Cryptographic signing is required
  only if the threat model and deployment boundary justify it.
- The release must use its normal prework and dependency-ordered slice plan.
