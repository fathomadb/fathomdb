counterfactual, minimum useful effect and allowed latency/cost/resource regression.

## Outcome and stopping rules

A selected study ACCEPTS only with valid complete data, the preregistered useful
held-out gain and every numerical guardrail satisfied. REJECT records valid
failure of the hypothesis; INCONCLUSIVE records valid insufficient evidence per
the preregistration. INVALID is no inferential verdict: wrong baseline/artifact,
leakage, incomplete cells or broken measurement prevents ACCEPT. A failed guardrail
cannot be averaged away by pooled recall/throughput.

STOP before launch for missing selection, exact inputs/access, owned setup,
predecessor qualification, reviewer PASS, approved venue/caps or tested long-run
resilience. During an authorized run stop on cap/deadline, eligibility/snapshot/
isolation violation, invalid completeness, unsafe resource behavior or unapproved
retry expansion. Preserve partial evidence and failures; no automatic restart,
extra cells, paid retry or test-set tuning. Product fixes require separate entry.

Bounded unchanged predecessor evidence retains its actual scope when source,
artifact/features, fixture/protocol and affected contracts remain applicable.
Explain reuse versus affected requalification; do not rerun a settled decision
or invent a confirming campaign. The [dependency record](status.md) identifies
which actual changes must be re-read before a later study.
