# Slice 2 disposition design

Artifact → inspect actual content/consumers → identify maintained versus
historical authority → preserve provenance → propose disposition → Slice 9
ruling → approved receiving slice. No age/name-based purge or branch-ancestry
substitute for behavioral/consumer proof. Prefer a small in-place annotation
where relocation offers little value.

Keep generated output under generator ownership, frozen progress as history,
contracts under accepted authority, and failed/negative evidence intact.
Absent local ignored payloads do not establish deletion elsewhere. No runtime
or public/schema surface is removed based on dead-code annotations alone.
