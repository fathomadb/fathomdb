# Slice 7 delivery design proposal

Retain cheap prerequisites → scoped feedback → final code-phase full gate →
affected package rehearsal → separately authorized publication → registry consumer
open/use/close/exit. No merge queue, new workflow family or broad repeat campaign.

Reuse evidence only when source, features, artifact bytes, configuration, platform
and relevant inputs match. A changed seam invalidates the corresponding proof,
not every historical result. Missing tools or scanner errors are blockers, not
clean scans. Use real exits and honest skipped/failed counts.

For .29 first-tag routing, prefer exact existing protections and candidate identity
checks. Either establish a permitted route or approve a narrowly scoped bootstrap
amendment. Preserve runner group selection, environment reviewers and held GPU
approval until normal routing passes. Do not widen to arbitrary future tags.

Preparation in 10 follows recorded 9 decisions. Expensive execution waits for
owned tools, sufficient approved capacity and affected venue/input availability.
Publication and changes to external runner/registry permissions stay separate.
