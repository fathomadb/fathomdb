These draft50/55/70/75 additions apply its methods as follows: anomaly testing→selected first/persistent commit/queue/provider faults and real reopened exact state; disabled optimization/independent oracle→same-snapshot reference route plus authored ranks/eligibility rather than two shared predicates; boundary/property/state machine→half-open validity, cap+1, NULL/zero, cursor/generation and small persistent-operation model; fuzz→actual boundary targets with size/time budgets and minimized regressions; resource leaks→owners/permits/fds/worker completion/temporary-WAL artifacts, not error-return-only tests; targeted mutations→a few plausible missing-cleanup/wrong-order/swallowed-error variants against chosen acceptance, retaining only useful regressions.

SQLite atomic transactions and integrity checks do not establish FathomDB canonical/projection/dependency/readiness correctness. SIGKILL/controlled process exit and reconstructed durable artifacts are different fault models; none establishes torn/reordered power-loss recovery. Prefer existing robust seams and bounded CPU fixtures. Do not add full-workspace100% line/branch/MC/DC, exhaustive VFS/OOM, generic B-tree/SQL fuzzers, large new harnesses or new infrastructure requirements. Runtime counts and matched evidence remain claims to establish. The added requirements are planned, not yet verified; unexecuted or unavailable proof stays open at70.
