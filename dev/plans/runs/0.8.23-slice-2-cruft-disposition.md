## Concrete follow-up proposals

1. **Current-authority navigation:** the 0.8.22 baseline identifies stale
   release-navigation pointers in `dev/plans/README.md`,
   `dev/plans/prompts/README.md`, `dev/DOC-INDEX.md`, and
   `dev/doc-index/plans.md`. After Slice 6, commission one bounded
   current-authority/index repair that updates these together; it must not
   rewrite closed boards or historical measurements.
2. **Historical measurement discrepancy:** `scripts/repo-prune/README.md` and
   its historical baseline/DELTA records disagree on a prior `runs/` count.
   A future prune-maintenance slice may reconcile the explanatory current
   document while preserving the original measurement provenance and its
   discrepancy note.
3. **Retention policy:** the growth of `dev/plans/runs/` is descriptive, not a
   removal authorization. Before any move/delete proposal, run the existing
   two-phase prune method, preserve path references, and obtain explicit HITL
   approval for the candidate set.
