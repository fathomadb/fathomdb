# 0.8.23 Slice 6 — hygiene and in-flight workplan

## Method

This workplan reviews every proposal in the Slice 6 preparation report. Value
is scored from 1 (limited release benefit) to 5 (material protection of user
behavior, release integrity, or evidence quality). Priority and sequence are
separate: dependencies, external lead time, blast radius, and the need to keep
the in-flight release hygienic can move a lower-value task ahead of a
higher-value one.

`P0` means perform at the next available bounded opportunity; `P1` means
prepare or execute in the first release wave once its prerequisite is ready;
`P2` means queue behind an external prerequisite or the first wave; `P3` means
retain as follow-on hygiene. Ratings are planning judgments, not feature scope
approval or an authorization to start the work.

higher-value external work, and defers high-value CUDA execution until its
evidence environment exists.

## Recurring gates

Every commissioned feature carries C23-03 and C23-04 regardless of its value
score or position in the sequence. Every public-surface change also carries
C23-01; Slice 30 additionally carries C23-02. A blocked external prerequisite
does not authorize substituting weaker evidence or expanding another slice.

This workplan prepares sequencing only. It does not change the requirement
that each task and feature receives an explicit bounded commission.
